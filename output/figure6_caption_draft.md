**Figure 6. Fitted variance composition under the K=3 diagonal-covariance model.** Bars partition each configured-group fit into its fitted between-run component and propagated within-run component; labels give exact support size n. Regimes are bookkeeping classes defined by the between-run fraction: at least 0.6, replicate-dominated; at most 0.4, sampling-dominated; otherwise balanced. With K=3, heterogeneity and the fitted variance components are weakly identified. Propagated group uncertainty assumes zero covariance among item estimates, so the composition is model-based and does not prescribe a causal remedy.
