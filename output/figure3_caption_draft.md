**Figure 3. Configured-group magnitude stability and profile coherence differ.** (A) Frozen magnitude rho for each of the 32 dataset-by-configured-group rows; each cell gives rho and the exact support size n. (B) Magnitude rho is shown against the implemented profile-coherence statistic, defined as the mean of the within-run sign-homogeneity values. Point area varies modestly with support size, and outlined points identify configured catalytic groups. The rho = 0.5 line is the provisional reference. The 0.6 line reproduces the value of the pooled-coherence eligibility reference and is drawn only as a visual guide. Signed-analysis eligibility is decided on pooled coherence at the selected support, so the profile-coherence values plotted here mark no eligibility decision, including for configured groups that were selected as supports. The lines define no validated quadrant. Profile coherence summarizes mean within-run sign homogeneity. It does not establish run-level sign recovery. Group-level uncertainty uses the zero-covariance approximation.
