**Figure 2 | Support-specific magnitude stability changes with support size.** (A) Observed item-level magnitude-stability statistics are compared with the corresponding first distinct configured-aggregate statistics for 924 frozen dataset-item profiles; the diagonal marks equality. (B) Observed first-aggregate-minus-item differences are shown descriptively by dataset. Exact support aliases were collapsed by membership before presentation. (C) Completed V9A K=3 zero-covariance synthetic characterizations show mean rho by support-size stratum, with 95% Monte Carlo intervals for draw-level means. Zero-mean item-to-first-aggregate increases exceeded 99.99% in every dataset template (minimum 99.9920%), and mean draw-level Spearman coefficients were positive under zero mean. V9A is a synthetic characterization, not a model of the upstream process. These results show that an aggregate increase is not evidence of recovered signal and that rho values at different supports describe support-specific objects. (D) First crossings across unique supports are shown only as sensitivity to four provisional operational references. V9A conditions on alias equivalence classes verified separately from frozen production memberships. Collapsed traversal is exact-support deduplication of named traversal, so equal selection follows structurally once duplicate representations share rho; it is not a Monte Carlo invariance rate. Production rho was equal across all 72,000 duplicate-representation checks. Collapsing aliases changed 672,379 of 924,000 reported item-decision labels.
