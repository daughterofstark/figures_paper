**Supplementary Figure S2 | Conditional selected-support signed associations under the implemented provisional screen.** The frozen screen contains 924 provisionally selected item rows representing 538 distinct selected fitted effects. Signed testing follows data-dependent first-crossing selection at provisional rho=0.5 and data-dependent pooled coherence eligibility at 0.6. (A) The 302 finite signed-test rows represent 268 distinct fitted effects; one fitted beta and its implemented Wald interval are shown per distinct fit. Square outlines mark fits copied to more than one selected item row. The stored within-dataset BH procedure flagged 152 item rows representing 145 distinct fitted effects. (B) All selected item-row locations are shown. Darker cells carry the stored BH-adjusted flag, negative estimates are hatched, gray cells have no finite signed test, blank cells are absent, and blue outlines mark copied fitted-effect representations. BH adjustment was implemented separately within each dataset over finite selected item rows. Support selection and coherence eligibility reuse the fitted input values; post-selection false-discovery-rate control has not been established, and Wald null calibration has not been independently validated for this selected pipeline. The figure is conditional and descriptive.
