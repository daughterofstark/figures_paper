**Figure 5 | Presence and provisional item-level recurrence vary across datasets.** Here, `n_total=4`; `n_present` is the number of datasets in which a coordinate exists; `n_absent=4-n_present`; `n_crossing` is the number of present datasets in which item rho meets the stated provisional reference; and `n_present_below_cutoff=n_present-n_crossing`. `crossing_all_four` requires `n_present=4` and `n_crossing=4`. (A) Explicit coordinate-dataset states at provisional rho=0.5 distinguish absence from presence below the reference and from crossing; side strips show the corresponding presence and crossing counts. (B) Joint counts by `n_present` and `n_crossing` show that crossing in every dataset where a coordinate is present (30) is not equivalent to being present and crossing in all four (18). (C) The strict all-four operational set changes across four predeclared provisional references. Absence is not treated as a below-reference observation. V9B validated deterministic counting and field semantics only; it did not validate homology or biological conservation. The cross-dataset coordinate mapping lacks an archived independent alignment artifact.
