**Figure 1. Observed item-level magnitude stability varies across coordinates.** (A) Frozen item-level rho values are shown separately for each dataset; grey cells denote coordinates absent from that dataset and are not rho=0. (B) The median uses only datasets in which each coordinate is present, with presence count retained below the axis. Open triangles mark the three catalytic-triad coordinates as orientation landmarks. Rho is a support-specific magnitude-stability statistic and does not encode sign agreement or probability of truth.
